# Legal, Consent and Policies (working draft. Have a lawyer/CA review before launch. This is not legal advice.)

## 1. Consent record (before any WhatsApp outreach)
Fields: lead_id, phone, channel (call), date-time, who asked (founder), seller's words ("haan bhej do"), purpose (send preview link + messages about it), revoked_at. Script line during call: "Main aapki dukaan ki website bana kar WhatsApp par bhej sakta hu? Aap kabhi bhi STOP likh kar band kar sakte hain." Store it. If seller says no, mark `lost`, never message.

## 2. Data we use for demos
Public business details (name, address, phone, rating, Maps link) are used only to build a preview for that business. We do not copy their Google/Instagram photos. Stock photos used until seller provides own. Delete lead/preview data on request within 7 days.

## 3. Seller terms (short, Hinglish + English)
- Vedhin Technology provides a hosted online shop, seller portal, and limited chatbot.
- **"Lifetime" means:** as long as the Vedhin platform operates, for the shop on our subdomain, within fair-use limits (storage, traffic, bot messages). Custom domain, extra storage, heavy traffic, and extra features are separate.
- We may change or discontinue the platform with 60 days' notice and will provide export of shop data (products, orders) in CSV.
- Support: first month free; after that Rs 499/month plan for bug support and bot usage; 2 free change-requests included in setup, then Rs 99 each.
- Seller is responsible for product listings, pricing, packing, shipping, returns, refunds, GST/tax and legal compliance. Vedhin is a technology provider, not the seller.
- Vedhin does not hold customer money. Payments go directly to the seller's UPI/bank. Payment confirmation is the seller's responsibility.
- No prohibited products (counterfeit brand goods, illegal items). We may suspend shops violating this.
- Refunds on setup fee: define a simple policy (e.g. within 7 days if not live).

## 4. Buyer-facing notices (on every shop)
Privacy notice: what we collect (name, phone, address) and that it's shared with the seller to fulfil the order. Seller-editable shipping/return policy. "Powered by Vedhin Technology."

## 5. Privacy / DPDP basics (India)
Collect minimum data, tell people why, keep it secure (encryption in transit, access control), allow deletion/correction on request, retention limits (e.g. delete unconverted lead data after 90 days), breach plan. Never send buyer personal data to free-tier AI providers.

## 6. WhatsApp policy
Follow Meta's WhatsApp Business policy: opt-in required, honor opt-outs, approved templates for business-initiated messages, no spam. Cap outreach (30/day), include STOP line, keep quality rating healthy, have a backup number plan.

## 7. Calls (TRAI)
Founder's personal one-to-one calls to businesses are fine; avoid automated/bulk promotional calls. Respect DND preferences. No auto-dialer in Phase 1.

## 8. Payments
No pooling of buyer money. Auto-gateway is seller's own account (their KYC). If Vedhin ever collects money on behalf of sellers, RBI payment aggregator rules apply: do not do this without a licensed partner.

## 9. Business basics checklist
Register Vedhin Technology properly (proprietorship/LLP/Pvt Ltd), GST if turnover/needs require, current bank account, invoices for setup fee, domain in company name, trademark search for "Vedhin" later.
