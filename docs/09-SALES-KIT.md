# Sales Kit (Hinglish)

## 1. Lead selection filter (ladies dress / jewellery, Jodhpur and Jaipur first)
Rating 4.0+, 30+ reviews, phone available, no website, shop 2+ years old, active on Instagram is a plus. Women-focused categories: suits, kurtis, sarees, lehenga, artificial/kundan/lac jewellery, boutiques, home-based Instagram sellers.

## 2. Call script (60-90 sec)
"Namaste, main {naam}, Vedhin Technology se bol raha hu. Maine aapki dukaan {shop} ke liye ek online shop ki free preview banayi hai, jisme aapka naam, logo aur products dikhte hain. Kya main WhatsApp par aapko link bhej sakta hu? Dekh ke koi badlav chahiye to wahin message kar dena, ho jayega. Aap kabhi bhi STOP likh kar band kar sakte hain."
After yes: log consent. If no: "Koi baat nahi, dhanyavaad." Mark lost.

## 3. First WhatsApp (after consent)
"Namaste {naam} ji, Vedhin Technology se. Aapki {shop} ki online preview: {link}. Isme kuch badalna ho (rang, logo, product, price) to yahin likh dijiye. Band karne ke liye STOP likhein."

## 4. Closing call (hot lead)
"Dekha aapne, aapki shop ki website, seller portal aur orders ka system sab ready hai. Ek baar ka Rs 1999, aur ye lifetime aapki rahegi (hamari subdomain par). Bug ya help ke liye Rs 499/month ka optional support, pehla mahina free. Aaj final kar dein to main aaj hi live kar deta hu."

## 5. Offer sheet (to send as image/PDF)
Rs 1999 one-time: shop (PWA) + seller portal + 20 products + QR/link + 2 free changes + guest checkout + UPI manual confirm + order tracking page. Optional Rs 499/month: support + bot + 2 changes/month. Add-ons: domain, Razorpay, extra template, festival banner, 3D hero.

## 6. Objection answers
- **"Mehnga hai":** "Ek baar ka Rs 1999, dukaan ka link 24 ghante khula rehta hai. 2-3 orders me paisa wapas."
- **"Mujhe samajh nahi aata":** "Aapko kuch seekhna nahi, photo WhatsApp par bhejiye, baaki hum karte hain. 30 sec ka video bhi hai."
- **"Instagram se hi bik jata hai":** "Instagram par DM me bhaw poochte rehte hain. Link bhejo, price, photo, order sab wahin. Aapka time bachega."
- **"Baad me sochungi":** "Preview aapke naam se tayar hai, jab bologe live kar denge. Kab call karun?"
- **"Monthly kyun?":** "Wo optional hai. Sirf support aur bot ke liye. Na lein to website chalti rahegi."
- **"Payment safe hai?":** "Customer ka paisa seedha aapke UPI me aata hai, hum beech me nahi aate."

## 7. Follow-up cadence
Day 0 call -> link. Day 2 one soft WhatsApp ("Kuch badalna ho to batayein"). Day 5 one call. After that stop unless seller engages. Max 2 follow-ups.

## 8. Referral / partners
Sales partners (photographers, boutique suppliers, Instagram page admins, courier agents): Rs 300-500 per paid seller. Seller referral: Rs 300 discount for both.

## 9. Help video scripts (30 sec each, in Hindi, screen recording)
1) Naya product kaise jodein 2) Order aaye to kya karein 3) Payment confirm kaise karein 4) Courier aur tracking ID kaise dalein 5) Shop ka link/QR kaise share karein 6) Rang aur logo kaise badlein.

## 10. Daily routine for founder
Morning: 10-15 calls. Afternoon: demos/edits, support. Evening: check panel, Telegram digest, plan next day's leads (40-50 pre-built).

## 11. Testimonials
First 10 sellers: offer Rs 499 pilot or free for a short video/testimonial and a "Vedhin" mention. Use only real, permissioned quotes.
